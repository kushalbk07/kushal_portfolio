How risk is calculated

River zones

Each zone combines two signals, and the higher one sets the level.

  • Flow index: the highest forecast river discharge in the next 7 days divided by the median of the previous 30 days.
  • Local rain: the heaviest forecast daily rainfall in the next 3 days near the monitoring point. Rain alone cannot push a river zone above High, because the river has not yet responded.

Cities & limitations

City markers are outlined by the same daily-rain thresholds, which are the commonly used heavy (64.5 mm) and very heavy (115.6 mm) rainfall categories. Heavy rain in hilly terrain raises flash-flood and landslide risk even where no large river is nearby.

  • River discharge comes from a global model (about 5 km resolution), not from gauge readings. Small rivers, such as the Bagmati in Kathmandu, are not resolved.
  • Monitoring points are approximate, and the flow index compares against the last 30 days, not a seasonal norm.
  • Weather refreshes about every 15 minutes at the source; river discharge updates daily. This page checks every 10 minutes.
