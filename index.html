PocketNepal: Expense Tracker & Bill Splitter

A free money tool built for Nepal, in Nepali Rupees. Split a bill equally or by custom amounts, work out who owes whom with the fewest possible payments, then track your spending against a monthly budget. No sign-up needed: everything is saved privately in your browser.

  • Bill splitter with tip, custom shares and smart settle-up
  • Expense tracker with search, filters, budget alerts and CSV export

Nepal Weather & Flood Risk Monitor

A real-time dashboard for Nepal that pulls live weather and river-discharge forecasts, then classifies flood risk for the country's major river basins on an interactive map. Includes 7-day river-flow charts, rainfall outlooks for key cities, and a transparent risk methodology.

  • Live weather for 13 cities across the Terai, Hills and Mountains
  • Four river-basin flood risk zones rated Low to Severe
  • Auto-refresh every 10 minutes, responsive on mobile
  • JavaScript
  • REST APIs
  • Leaflet
  • Data Visualisation
  • Responsive UI
